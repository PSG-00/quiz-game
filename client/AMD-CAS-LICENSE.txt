Kernel source: https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-CAS/blob/master/ffx-cas/ffx_cas.h
This project adapts the five-sample fast sharpen-only kernel with gamma-2
input/output approximation. The optional lower-resolution mode combines
bilinear upsampling with this kernel; it is not AMD EASU/RCAS or full FSR.
